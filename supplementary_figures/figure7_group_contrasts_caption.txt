Difference between group means of paired participant MAE changes. Negative values favor the first named group. Independent participant resampling within each group preserves baseline/post pairing through the change scores. 95% BCa intervals are exploratory and pointwise, not Tukey-adjusted intervals. These estimates complement, and do not replace, the earlier ANOVA/Tukey analysis. Crossing zero cannot demonstrate equal efficacy; absence of a no-training group also limits causal interpretation of pre/post improvement.
