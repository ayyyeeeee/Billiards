Figure 4. Paired baseline and post-test angle–strength phase space in the both task. Each point is one participant’s mean signed angle and strength over the same jointly valid trials; gray segments connect that participant’s two stages. Baseline points and dashed ellipses are gray; post-test points and solid ellipses use the training-group color. Crosses mark group centers and arrows show baseline-to-post-test center displacement. Each coordinate is divided by the pooled baseline across-participant sample SD for that measure, without subtracting its mean, so the origin remains the zero-error target. Identical symmetric axes and equal aspect are used. Ellipses use sample covariance and the chi-square(2) 0.95 contour as a bivariate-normal reference distribution ellipse. They describe dispersion of participant means; they are not 95% confidence regions for the group mean or within-person trial variability. Area change is 100 × (post-test area / baseline area − 1); positive values mean expansion. Variance ratios refer to normalized angle variance divided by normalized strength variance. Center shift is Euclidean distance in normalized coordinates. These descriptive changes do not by themselves establish learning, contraction, or angle–strength decoupling. All points and complete ellipse boundaries are included. Original-unit coordinates and normalization factors are supplied in the accompanying CSV files.
