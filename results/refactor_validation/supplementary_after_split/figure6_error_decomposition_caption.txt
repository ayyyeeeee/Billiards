Descriptive decomposition of observed mean squared error (MSE), with target zero. For each participant and stage, MSE = mean(value)^2 + variance(value, ddof=0). Paired changes in the two terms sum exactly to the paired MSE change. Bars show equally weighted participant means; diamonds show total MSE change and participant-resampled 95% BCa CI. Component intervals are in the CSV table. Small baseline sample size increases sampling error of the empirical signed mean. A sensitivity table therefore also partitions MSE as (mean^2 - s^2/n) + s^2; this bias correction assumes identically distributed, independent trials and may produce negative squared-bias estimates. Neither partition isolates intrinsic motor noise. Capped recorded scores remain capped, so these are observed-score moments.
